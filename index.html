Questions, answered

No — it's a roleplay airline inside Roblox. You book seats, board real toolbox aircraft, fly to rebuilt destination airports and earn Rhys Credits. Every rank, from Passenger to CEO, is earned in-game.
Pick a destination on this site and we'll hold a seat for you (or book directly in-game from the Flights tab). Fares are charged in Rhys Credits in-game, or shown in AUD here for your convenience. London is 5,000 — so start with Adelaide at 600.
Head to your bay (A1–A4) before pushback, walk up to the aircraft and press E at the door. Your boarding pass lives in MY TICKETS — with barcode, seat and gate.
Absolutely. Pass the training quizzes in the Career tab to become Ground Crew, Check-in Agent, Cabin Crew or ATC — each pays RC per shift. Supervisors can promote staff; pilots are appointed by management.
Your Rhys Credits, rank, tickets, achievements and flight history persist to your Roblox account across sessions — and your web bookings live in this browser, findable by PNR on the My Bookings page.
